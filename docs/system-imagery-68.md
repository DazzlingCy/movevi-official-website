## Prompts

### plan

Photorealistic editorial sports lifestyle photograph for a premium running website, portrait 4:5. A candid close crop of a real adult runner preparing today's run at a dark walnut table beside a window, only relaxed forearms and hands visible, a paper city street map with a modest blue pen line and a small handwritten weekly training notebook, black running shoes at the lower edge and a simple water bottle. Composition should clearly communicate route and workout planning through everyday physical objects, NOT a tabletop miniature city or sci-fi dashboard. Quiet natural overcast daylight, authentic imperfect paper grain, realistic unretouched skin, deep charcoal and muted blue-grey tones with gentle warm wood, restrained contrast, documentary photography, 50mm lens, plausible shallow depth of field. One coherent real scene, no floating objects, no neon, no hologram, no 3D rendering, no logos, no slogans, no legible dense text, no graphic overlays. Fill frame with real photograph, no border.

### ai

Photorealistic editorial lifestyle photograph for a premium fitness website, portrait 4:5.
