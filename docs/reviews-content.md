# User review section

Added after hardware products and before values, inside chapter 08 (`#reviews`).

The owner explicitly approved clearly labelled example reviews on 2026-10-16. All three quotes and role descriptions are fictional editorial examples, not collected customer statements. They must not be presented as verified reviews or used in review/rating structured data. Disclosure appears in the heading, each card and the section footer. No ratings, customer counts or invented names are included.

Photos reuse existing assets: TS3 home and control-area product photography, plus the Paris running scene poster. These are illustrative scenes, not portraits of the speakers, as noted on the images.

To replace examples, obtain actual customer quotes and permission to publish, then update the quote, attribution and any approved photo in `dist/index.html`. Remove example labels only for verified replacements; retain the section disclosure while any examples remain.

Presentation lives in `dist/reviews.css`. Desktop uses three equal columns, mobile stacks cards. Entrance uses the site's existing `data-reveal` behavior with reduced-motion support.
